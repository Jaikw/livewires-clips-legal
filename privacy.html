Privacy Policy

Livewires Clips Tool · Effective October 27, 2026

Livewires Clips Tool is a small private tool used by one person, Jaik Wolfe, to post short video clips to the TikTok account @livewires.clips. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool does with TikTok

The tool connects to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official developer interfaces. It uploads video clips the owner has made, with a title and caption, so the owner can review and publish them.

Information we handle

  • The access token TikTok gives the tool when the owner connects the account. It is stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • The video files, titles and captions the owner chooses to post.

The tool does not collect information from any other TikTok users, visitors or viewers.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share information with anyone. The only place data is sent is TikTok, to publish the owner's own clips.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from TikTok's app settings, and can delete the stored token and files from their own computer.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the owner through the TikTok account @livewires.clips.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
